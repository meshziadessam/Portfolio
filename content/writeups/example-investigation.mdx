## Scope

This example uses a fictional account and synthetic log entries.

## Observations

```json
{"time":"09:10Z","event":"failed sign-in","account":"training-user"}
{"time":"09:14Z","event":"successful sign-in","account":"training-user"}
```

The two records share an account name. Additional context would be needed to determine whether the events came from the same device or session.

## Lessons learned

Record what the evidence shows and document which details still need verification.
